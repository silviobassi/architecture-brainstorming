# References and their limits

Baseline consulted during the initial draft on November 2, 2026. Verify applicable editions and content
when a concrete decision depends on them. Prefer primary sources to third-party summaries.

## Engineering and description

- [ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html): requirements-engineering processes
  and information across the lifecycle. The consulted catalog reports a revision in progress. Use
  the applicable published edition; a draft revision does not automatically supersede a published standard.
- [ISO/IEC 25010:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html): product quality model. It can guide
  discovery and assessment; it does not make every characteristic mandatory in every project.
- [ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/74393.html): architecture descriptions and
  related concepts. It prescribes neither a particular architecture, design method, nor document format.
- [IREB — official CPRE materials](https://cpre.ireb.org/en/downloads-and-resources/downloads): foundations,
  elicitation, modeling, and requirements management. Select material relevant to the activity.
- [INCOSE — Requirements Working Group](https://www.incose.org/group/requirements-working-group/):
  needs and requirements guidance, including the Guide to Writing Requirements. Consult available
  material; do not attribute specific rules to full texts that have not been accessed.

Public ISO abstracts were consulted during creation. Full standards are not included in this skill.
It is not a certified procedure and does not establish clause-by-clause compliance.

## Quality and architectural assessment

- [SEI — Quality Attribute Workshop](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/quality-attribute-workshop-collection/):
  scenario elicitation, prioritization, and refinement, including before detailed architecture exists.
- [SEI — eliciting quality attribute requirements](https://sei.cmu.edu/documents/5465/2013_018_101_60984.pdf):
  scenarios containing source, stimulus, environment, affected artifact, response, and measure.
- [SEI — scenario-based architectural analysis](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/scenario-based-analysis-of-software-architecture/):
  reference for assessing architecture through scenarios and qualities.

Adapting SEI techniques does not mean executing a complete formal assessment method.

## Documentation practices

- [MADR](https://adr.github.io/madr/): community templates for decisions and rationale, including lightweight versions.
- [Rust — official RFC template](https://github.com/rust-lang/rfcs/blob/main/0000-template.md): proposals
  with motivation, alternatives, drawbacks, and open questions. Adapt project-specific content.
- [Atlassian — PRDs](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/requirements/): product practice
  covering goals, assumptions, stories, and shared understanding. Not a universal standard.

The templates in this skill are original, adaptable syntheses, not complete reproductions of these
materials. Established practices can be more appropriate than recent techniques; novelty does not prove precision.

## Specialized sources on demand

For security, privacy, regulation, and other domains, find the relevant authority or maintainer,
verify jurisdiction, edition, and context, and distinguish obligations from recommendations.
Do not make one country's laws or one vendor's architecture permanent rules for every project.
